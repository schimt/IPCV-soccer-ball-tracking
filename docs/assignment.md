Task: Soccer ball tracking
Purpose / learning objectives: Get experience with data annotation, method selection, implementation, and evaluation.

Requirements:
* Choose and implement both a non-NN and a NN-based method for tracking the ball in play.
* You may use the data from SoccerNet for training (go to Tracking data and labels): https://www.soccer-net.org/data
* Annotate and evaluate on a new 1-minute clip from a different environment: Either choose your own video clip from your favourite club, or take the clip we provide below.

Hand-in (individual):
Submit your code, your video file (in case you did not use the video from Moodle), and your annotations.
Submit a pdf with text answering the following questions:
* Briefly justify why you chose the methods you did
* How did you train your models? Include description of which training data you used, and why.
* How did you annotate the test data (which tool, etc.)?
* What were the pros and cons of the NN-based vs. non-NN-based methods you implemented?
* How would you expect your model performs for ball tracking in other sports (volleyball, golf, ...)?
* Please declare your use of Generative AI for the project

Evaluation:
We will use a peer-review process on Moodle, where each of you review two other submissions and give brief feedback.

Expected workload:
50 hours are assigned for the mini-project, including the evaluation.

Deadline:
November 16.
